The slopes of Mount Kilimanjaro are her office. She belongs to the Chagga tribe,
a Bantu-speaking community traditionally concentrated on the mountain’s
fertile southern and eastern foothills. Their livelihoods are closely
connected to tourism and farming, especially coffee and bananas.

<Photo src="IMG-20240307.webp" caption="The sun also rises" />

In 2022 she successfully completed training on Mountain Tour Guiding at Mweka 
College of African Wildlife Management.

<Gallery photos={["IMG-20220802.webp", "IMG_20230721_171833.webp"]} />

She is not only qualified mountain guide, but also very good person, 
daughter, and mother. Whether she is tired or stressed, she still gives her 
children care, patience, and love. The kindness, patience, and love she shows 
as a mother are a reflection of what she learned from her own mama. 
I’m grateful for her mother, because she raised an incredible daughter.

<Gallery photos={["IMG-20230425.webp", "IMG-20231010.webp"]} />

A lot of coffee is grown there beneath shade trees. Still, Chagga 
traditional drink is banana beer. Mbege is more than a beverage: 
it is associated with weddings, funerals and business discussions.
