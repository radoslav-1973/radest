The slopes of Mount Kilimanjaro are our home. I belong to the Chagga tribe,
a Bantu-speaking community traditionally concentrated on the mountain’s
fertile southern and eastern foothills. Our livelihoods are closely
connected to tourism and farming, especially coffee and bananas.

<Photo src="IMG-20240307.webp" caption="The sun also rises" />

In 2022, I successfully completed training on Mountain Tour Guiding at Mweka 
College of African Wildlife Management.

<Gallery photos={["IMG-20220802.webp", "IMG_20230721_171833.webp"]} />

I hope my children will remember that their mama tried. Even when I was tired, 
even when I was stressed, even when I was scared and had no clue what to do, 
I had every intention of being the best mother I could be, and I hope they know, 
I did it because I dearly love them with all my heart.

<Gallery photos={["IMG-20230425.webp", "IMG-20231010.webp"]} />

Here around Kilimanjaro, there is plenty of coffee growing beneath shade trees, 
but our Chagga traditional drink is banana beer. Mbege is more than a beverage: 
it is associated with weddings, funerals and business discussions.
